Our story

A quiet obsession with brilliance

Glitter Macedonia began with a simple idea: that sparkle can be sophisticated. We source fine cosmetic glitters, chrome powders, flakes and gels — then strip everything else away, so the finish speaks for itself.

Today we supply nail artists, makeup professionals and enthusiasts across the country with a tightly edited catalogue and honest pricing.
